Mandatory Requirements

What your Tableau system must demonstrate

Time intelligence
  • Month-over-Month absolute variation.
  • Month-over-Month growth %.
  • Year-over-Year comparison.
  • Moving average, minimum 3 periods.
  • At least 1 trendline.
  • Time-based line chart.
  • Time-based comparison bar chart.
Ranking and Top analysis
  • Dynamic Top N using a parameter.
  • Bump Chart showing rank evolution over time.
  • Sorted bar chart.
  • Static Top filters are not accepted.
Distribution and structure
  • Pareto Chart with cumulative %.
  • Box Plot.
  • Highlight Table with totals displayed.
  • At least one additional distribution visualization, such as a histogram.
  • Highlight Tables must use meaningful color scales and show structural strengths or weaknesses.
Relationships and clustering
  • Scatter Plot.
  • Tableau Clustering.
  • Interpretation of clusters.
  • Meaningful size encoding.
  • Meaningful color logic.
  • Clusters must be explained in business terms.
LOD expressions, groups and sets
  • Minimum 3 FIXED calculations.
  • LOD expressions must be logically correct.
  • Create at least one meaningful Group.
  • Use the Group to simplify analysis or improve clarity.
  • Include at least one dynamic Set, such as Top N or threshold-based.
Filters, parameters and actions
  • Demonstrate Dimension Filters, Measure Filters and Context Filters.
  • Show at least one cascading filter, such as Region to Category to Sub-Category, adapted to your dataset.
  • Use Context filters intentionally.
  • Include at least 3 parameters.
  • Parameters must control multiple visualizations, update titles dynamically and be integrated into calculated fields.
  • Include at least 1 Filter Action and 1 Highlight Action.
  • Static dashboards will be penalized.
