The brief

Build a connected analytical system for strategic review.

Management needs more than three attractive dashboards. It needs evidence about growth, business structure and strategic position, with interactions that help users move from signal to diagnosis and decision.

Assessment rules

Required components are fixed. Analytical choices must be justified.

Make defensible choices.

Select measures and dimensions because they answer the business question.

Configure calculations correctly.

Addressing, partitioning, period logic and grain must survive validation.

Use real interaction.

Static dashboards lose marks. Actions must connect the analytical system.

Dashboard 2

Distribution & Concentration

Expose revenue concentration, variability, outliers and structural strengths or weaknesses.

Required
Pareto chartWho creates most of the result?

Sort contribution descending and add cumulative percentage.

Validation

The final cumulative mark must reach 100%. Check the contribution grain before interpreting the 80% point.

BoxplotHow variable are observations?

Choose a meaningful observation grain before adding the distribution.

Validation

Explain the median, IQR and at least one decision-relevant outlier. Unusual does not automatically mean incorrect.

Highlight tableWhere does the pattern concentrate?

Use two dimensions and a colour scale appropriate to the measure.

Validation

Use a diverging scale only when a meaningful midpoint exists. Sort to reveal structure.

Your analytical choices
Dimension

Region, Category, Sub-Category or Segment

Measure

Choose the metric that makes concentration and variability meaningful

Grain

Customer, order, product or another defensible observation

Distribution check

A boxplot uses raw Superstore rows while the analysis claims to compare orders. What must change?

Dashboard 2 must answer

Where is value concentrated, which group is most variable and which outlier or structural weakness deserves action?

Dashboard 3

Ranking, Relationships & Segmentation

Explain competitive position, multi-measure patterns and strategic opportunity or risk.

Required
Bump chartHow does rank evolve?

Rank within each time period and reverse the axis so rank 1 appears at the top.

Validation

Ranks must restart inside every period. Explain one rise, decline or stable position.

Scatter plot + clustersWhich patterns form segments?

Declare the grain of each dot, add meaningful measures and apply Tableau clustering.

Validation

Describe each cluster using its actual measure profile. Do not treat the automatic cluster number as a business interpretation.

Strategic choiceWhat additional view closes the decision?

Choose a chart because it answers the remaining question, not because it looks advanced.

Justification

Name the comparison, the audience and the decision this view improves.

Cluster interpretation

Tableau labels a cluster “Cluster 2”. What belongs in the business story?

Dashboard 3 must answer

Who gained or lost position, what relationship or cluster matters, and which strategic response follows?

System requirements

The three dashboards must behave as one analytical product.

3+Dashboard actions

Filter, highlight, navigation or another purposeful action.

1+Parameter

It must change a measure, threshold, view or decision state.

3+Table calculations

Correctly configured and validated in context.

1Connected experience

Consistent navigation, filters and visual language.

Action 1Overview to diagnosis

Select a mark to filter a supporting view.

Action 2Preserve comparison

Highlight a series or segment without removing context.

Action 3Move through the system

Navigate between dashboards or reveal controlled detail.

ParameterLet the decision question change

Switch measure, Top N, threshold or analytical perspective.

Parameter quality

A parameter appears on the dashboard but changing it has no visible effect. Does it satisfy the requirement?

Interaction test script
  1. Open the workbook in its default state.
  2. Use every action and parameter once.
  3. Clear the selection and confirm recovery.
  4. Check titles, KPI values and captions reflect the active state.
  5. Repeat after publishing to Tableau Public.

Assessment review

Review the workbook through the same lenses used for evaluation.

Analytical thinkingThe question, grain and comparison make business sense.
  • Claims are specific
  • Choices are justified
  • Limits are acknowledged
Table calculationsDirection, partitions and period logic are correct.
  • Manual spot-checks agree
  • Ranks restart correctly
  • Filters do not silently break results
Visual choicesEach form matches the required comparison.
  • Encoding is accurate
  • Chart grain is declared
  • Complexity earns its place
Business interpretationEvidence leads to decisions rather than descriptions.
  • Insights use measures and periods
  • Clusters receive meaningful names
  • Recommendations are actionable
Clean designHierarchy, formatting and accessibility support scanning.
  • Consistent titles and units
  • Colour has a stable meaning
  • No clipped or redundant objects
Interactive logicActions and parameters improve diagnosis.
  • State remains visible
  • Reset behaviour works
  • Published interactions are tested
